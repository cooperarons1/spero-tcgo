## Proposed adjustments — 10 changes

**No card edits in this commit.** This table is the proposal — user
signs off on a subset, then a follow-up commit makes the actual
`data/cards.json` changes.

| # | cardCode | Type | Change | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | NEU107 The Crucible's Heart | NERF | 10 mana → 12 mana, OR keep cost and reduce body 8/8 → 6/6 | 88% WR is game-ending. Delay by 2 turns or weaken the body. |
| 2 | NEU106 Gavalon Sandwyrm | NERF | Add downside (next-turn cost+2), OR reduce stats by 1/-1 | 87% WR on a Neutral with no synergy tax is too good. |
| 3 | JIM030 Infernic | NERF | Cost 4 → 5, OR -1 attack | Over-statted for cost. |
| 4 | JIM032 Nova Ramiro | NERF | Health 5 → 4, OR add "takes 1 damage each turn" | Aggressive scaling too high. |
| 5 | TAL037 Snowball, Alpha Fox | WATCH/NERF | Gate the buff: only fires if a beast was played this turn | Small sample but consistent 76% WR — re-check after 50K more games. |
| 6 | DRK033 Quick Fix | BUFF | Cost 1 → 0, OR effect: heal 5 + 2 armor | Derek needs early answers. |
| 7 | DRK035 Recall | BUFF | Cost 2 → 1, AND draw a card on cast | Worst card in the game. |
| 8 | DRK034 Reassign | REWORK | New effect: give friendly minion +0/+2 and Taunt | Current effect unclear; reframe as defensive. |
| 9 | JIM038 Warning Shot | BUFF | Damage 1 → 2 | Mulligan-bait that doesn't pull its weight. |
| 10 | TAL031 Plup | BUFF | Stats 2/2 → 2/3, OR "costs 0 if you played a beast last turn" | Weak body; either buff stats or add tempo hook. |

**Net:** 5 nerfs (mostly Jimmy + neutrals), 5 buffs (all Derek + 2 mulligan baits).

## Open questions for the user

1. **Should we regenerate the balance sim against Run C2 weights first?**
   The current data was generated against the broken Run C neural eval
   with the wrong HERO_CLASSES one-hot. DEREK's collapse is plausibly
   *partially* an artifact of that. A 250K-game re-sim against the
   shipping Run C2 weights would clarify whether DEREK is actually
   broken or just looked broken because of bad training data.
   **Recommended.** ETA ~20 min on the M5.

2. **If DEREK is still broken after the re-sim**, do we:
   - (a) Ship a "DEREK universal +1 stat / -1 cost" patch as commit
     to drag the class up from ~20% toward ~40% in one move, OR
   - (b) Spec a new core mechanic for DEREK (rework, ~weeks of design),
     OR
   - (c) Ship the 5 specific DRK card buffs in this audit and re-test

3. **Which of the 10 proposed changes do you want to ship?**
   The table is sized to be a single buildable patch but each change
   is independent. Some are obvious (NEU107 88% WR is undeniable),
   some are judgment calls (TAL037 has a small sample).

4. **The mulligan outliers are a different kind of fix:** they're not
   under/over-powered — they're *misleadingly framed*. Stat buffs are
   the cheapest fix; UI hints (e.g. "rarely kept" tag in mulligan)
   are a more elegant alternative if we want to teach instead of
   force.
